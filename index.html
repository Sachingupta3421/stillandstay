My story

It started with one stay.

I still remember walking into a five-star hotel for the first time. The calm of the lobby, the way everything seemed to have been thought about, a room that felt like it had been waiting for me. I couldn’t stop taking pictures. It was a little crazy, in the best way.

Since then I’ve stayed at places like the Novotel in Kerala and the JW Marriott, and that feeling never got old. What stayed with me wasn’t the size of the room. It was the small moments that made me want to come back.

I’ve always loved photography and filmmaking. So I asked myself: why not use it for the hotels that make people feel this way? The ones that don’t just offer a stay, but give guests a reason to return.

Great hotels don’t just sell a room. They give you a reason to come back. My job is to make sure the world can see it.

— Sachin, Still & Stay

The details

Guests remember the small things.

The light caught in a row of glasses. Bath amenities lined up on a lit shelf. The first coffee by the window. I slow down for the details that tell guests how a place will feel, and give them the same care as the wide shots.

07:00
  1. 07:00

    First light

    Mist lifting off the mountains, the balcony chairs still empty. Soft, low sun for terraces, views and breakfast.

  2. 12:00

    High sun

    The pool at its brightest blue. Midday is for water, terraces and anything that should look fresh and clean.

    Even light through the tall windows and across the painted walls. The hour for rooms and suites, styled bed by bed.
